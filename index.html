About the Firm

Fifteen Years of Trust, Built One Client at a Time

R.S. Kapoor & Co. was established in 2008 by CA Rajesh Kapoor, with a singular purpose — to bring reliable, jargon-free financial guidance to businesses and individuals who deserve more than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Today, our team of qualified CAs and tax professionals serves over 500 clients across manufacturing, trading, services, and salaried individuals. We specialize in making compliance straightforward, so your books are always clean and your filings are always on time.

We are registered members of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) and carry professional indemnity insurance for every engagement we undertake.
